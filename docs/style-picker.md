# Style & Aesthetic Picker

One-click artistic movements, illustration styles, and color harmonies for ComfyUI.

## Overview

The **ModusFlow Style & Aesthetic Picker** (`ModusFlowStylePicker`) provides instant, curated aesthetic presets. Instead of guessing prompt syntax or trying to remember obscure artist names, users can choose from 14 signature aesthetics and 7 color palette harmonies.

## Curated Presets

### Art Movements & Fine Art
- **`dark_fantasy_oil`**: Heavy impasto brushwork, dramatic baroque lighting, Frank Frazetta aesthetic.
- **`watercolor_ink_wash`**: Fluid translucent pigment pooling on cold-press cotton paper.
- **`ukiyo_e_woodblock`**: Traditional Japanese woodblock contours, decorative flat color blocks, Washi texture.
- **`art_nouveau_mucha`**: Sinuous flowing curves, golden botanical filigree, Alphonse Mucha inspired.
- **`surrealist_dreamscape`**: Dali-inspired dreamscapes, impossible physics, floating architecture.

### Animation & Illustration
- **`90s_retro_anime`**: Hand-drawn cel shading, analog grain, nostalgic Studio Ghibli vibes.
- **`shinkai_cinematic`**: Makoto Shinkai style, breathtaking skies, brilliant god rays.
- **`comic_book_noir`**: Gritty graphic novel lineart, heavy cross-hatching, dramatic noir shadows.
- **`pixar_3d_stylized`**: Soft tactile subsurface scattering, expressive proportions, Octane render feel.
- **`claymation_stop_motion`**: Authentic plasticine clay surface texture, subtle handmade sculpting marks.
- **`cyberpunk_synthwave`**: Futuristic neon holographic glow, electric cyan and hot magenta.

### Editorial & Photography
- **`vogue_fashion_editorial`**: High-fashion avant-garde styling, studio elegance, clean lines.
- **`national_geographic`**: Tack-sharp wildlife realism, natural color grading.
- **`sci_fi_concept_art`**: Epic matte painting scale, industrial architecture, Syd Mead inspired.

## Color Palettes
- `warm_golden_amber`: Luminous honey tones and warm shadows.
- `cool_cyber_cyan`: Electric teal, cyan, and deep oceanic navy.
- `monochrome_high_contrast`: Stark black & white with silver mid-tones.
- `pastel_dreamy`: Delicate lavender, mint, and powder pink.
- `moody_desaturated`: Muted slate, charcoal, and somber tones.
- `vibrant_hyper_saturated`: Punchy primary hues with dynamic pop-art intensity.

Outputs structured `style_tags`, `negative_tags`, `full_prompt`, and passes through the `pipe`.
